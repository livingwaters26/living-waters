I Will Shake All Nations (Haggai 2:6–9)

The nations shaken; the latter glory of this house greater than the former.
ASV
6For thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth, and the sea, and the dry land; 7and I will shake all nations; and the precious things of all nations shall come; and I will fill this house with glory, saith Jehovah of hosts. 8The silver is mine, and the gold is mine, saith Jehovah of hosts. 9The latter glory of this house shall be greater than the former, saith Jehovah of hosts; and in this place will I give peace, saith Jehovah of hosts.
Premillennial
A future shaking of the nations preceding Christ's return and the literal Millennial temple's glory.
Amillennial
Fulfilled at Christ's first coming; Hebrews 12:26–27 applies the "shaking" directly to the New Covenant surpassing the old.
Postmillennial
The nations' shaking pictures historical upheavals as Christ's kingdom advances and eclipses every rival order.
