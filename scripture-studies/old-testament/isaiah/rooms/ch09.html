ICultural & Historical Context+

Delivered against the backdrop of Assyrian invasion and the collapse of the Northern Kingdom's political hopes, this oracle promised a ruler from David's own line — not a foreign deliverer, but one raised up from within the covenant people themselves.

IICore Theological Meaning+

Four royal titles, each bearing weight no ordinary king could carry — "Mighty God" and "Everlasting Father" push this figure beyond the category of a merely human monarch, while "Prince of Peace" answers directly to a nation exhausted by war.

IIICross-References & Canonical Links+

Matthew 4:15–16 applies the "great light" of 9:2 directly to the beginning of Jesus' Galilean ministry. Luke 1:32–33 echoes the throne-of-David, no-end language almost verbatim in Gabriel's announcement to Mary.
