✍️ Author Profile — Jude

Who wrote it, when, and to whom.

Identity
Jude, "brother of James" (v.1) — traditionally understood as a half-brother of Jesus and of James, leader of the Jerusalem church, though he humbly declines to claim that relationship to Jesus directly.
Time Period
Likely c. 65-80 AD; its close overlap with 2 Peter 2 raises a well-known, still-debated literary-dependency question between the two letters.
Target Audience
Jewish Christians, given the letter's heavy reliance on Old Testament and Jewish apocalyptic tradition, facing infiltration by false teachers twisting grace into license for immorality.
Historical Context — Empire & Era
Written during a period of rising heterodox teaching in the early church, predating or roughly contemporary with the more developed 2nd-century Gnostic systems the church fathers would later combat at length.
