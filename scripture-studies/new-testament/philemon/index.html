✍️ Author Profile — Philemon

Who wrote it, when, and to whom.

Identity
The Apostle Paul, "a prisoner of Jesus Christ," joined by Timothy; addressed to Philemon, a wealthy Colossian Christian and house-church host, along with Apphia and Archippus.
Time Period
c. 60-62 AD, during Paul's Roman imprisonment — one of the four "Prison Epistles" alongside Ephesians, Philippians, and Colossians, likely carried by the same messenger, Tychicus (cf. Col 4:7-9).
Target Audience
Philemon personally, plus his household, regarding Onesimus — Philemon's runaway slave, converted after encountering Paul in Rome.
Historical Context — Empire & Era
Written under Emperor Nero, during Paul's first Roman imprisonment; Roman slavery's severe legal treatment of recovered fugitives forms the entire backdrop of the letter's careful, indirect appeal.
