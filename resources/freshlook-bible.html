Joshua Smith · 15-Year Project

The Bible Text, Visually Optimized

"The way we look at text on a page — or a screen — affects the way we understand it, and how we're able to teach it and present it to others."
— Joshua Smith, FreshLook Bible

It's not a new Bible or a new translation. It's the same King James Bible text — but laid out according to what every passage actually is. Poetry formatted as poetry. Lists as lists. Dialogue as dialogue. Genealogies as genealogies. Speeches and letters and law codes each given the visual treatment their content demands.

Smith went through every passage and every book and asked one question: what should this text look like according to what it is? After 15 years, that project is complete — and available in multiple formats.

Available Formats

Six Ways to Engage Scripture

Format 1

Digital Panorama

Every book of the Bible on its own page — the complete text, fully laid out. Zoom in to a comfortable reading size and scroll straight through, or zoom out to see the book's entire structure at a glance.

  • Table of contents — navigate between all 66 books instantly
  • Tap column bottom to jump to the next column
  • Color-coded by content type throughout
Format 2

Printed Panorama

Each book on a single large sheet — the whole book visible at once so you can see how its outline and argument hold together from first verse to last. Galatians' three major sections. Daniel's interlocking visions. All of it on one page.

  • Rigid PVC substrate — lays completely flat, fully durable
  • Also available as large posters
  • Every book of the Bible available in this format
Format 3

Printed Books

All the FreshLook formatting available as printed books. Get Bible sections or individual books, in thinner or thicker editions — ideal for Sunday school classes, small groups, or personal study.

  • Thinner individual books — buy multiple copies for a class
  • Thicker collections for personal reading and deep study
  • Study and reading editions available
Ministry Resource

FreshLook Outreach Maps

Large laminated wall maps built for soul-winning and outreach. Satellite imagery detailed enough to see individual houses and read street names clearly — dry-erase surface so you can mark up progress and erase as you go.

  • Customized to your specific area — neighborhood, town, or county
  • Your logo, Bible verse, banner, scale, and size
  • Track progress visually as you reach a neighborhood with the Gospel

FreshLook Calendars

Finger-style acoustic guitar recordings of hymns — worshipful, unhurried background music for study or personal reflection. Available as physical CDs and digital downloads. Listen to samples at freshlookbible.com.

How This Connects to Your Study

FreshLook Bible's core idea — that the way text is laid out changes how you understand it — runs parallel to the approach this whole site is built on. The Printed Panoramas are especially powerful alongside the verse-by-verse commentary rooms here: seeing a whole book's structure on one page changes how individual chapters read.

Use the Panorama to see where a passage sits inside the whole book. Use the commentary rooms here to go deep on that passage. The two work together.
